YT Publisher – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

YT Publisher is a private tool used only by its owner to manage his own YouTube channels. It has no other users.

Data accessed

With the owner's authorization through Google OAuth, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload and manage the owner's own videos, playlists, captions and comments. It does not request or access data of any other person.

Storage

Authorization tokens and video metadata are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's APIs.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to third parties. No analytics, advertising or tracking is used.

Google API Services

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also uses YouTube API Services, subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: the support e-mail shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
