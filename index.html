YT Publisher

Private tool · personal use only

YT Publisher is a private, single-user tool that its owner runs on his own computer to publish videos to his own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it does, using the YouTube Data API on the owner's own channels:

  • uploads the owner's finished videos and schedules their publication,
  • sets titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions and playlists,
  • posts the owner's planned comment under his own videos after they go public.

It does not access anyone else's channel or data.
